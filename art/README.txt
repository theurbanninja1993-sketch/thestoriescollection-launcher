# Box art

Drop the two covers here, named to match `art=` in `launcher.ini`:

    art/vcs.png    Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Stories
    art/lcs.png    Grand Theft Auto: Liberty City Stories

**Nothing is shipped here on purpose** — the covers are Rockstar's artwork, so
they are the one thing the launcher asks you to supply yourself. Use the
front-cover image you own (a scan, or a store front image), roughly the PS2
cover shape (about 1:1.4, portrait).

Until a file exists the card draws a placeholder saying `BOX ART` with the
filename it is waiting for, so you can see exactly what to drop in and where.

The image is fitted inside the card without distorting its aspect ratio, so a
slightly different shape is fine — it is centred, not stretched.
